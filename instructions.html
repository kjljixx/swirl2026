Here are the rules of chess: Wikipedia

In short,

  • 2 Colors: White & Black
  • Each player starts with 16 pieces: 8 pawns; 2 rooks, knights, bishops each; 1 queen; 1 king
  • Each piece has its own movement. Pawns can move 1 square forward, and capture diagonally 1 square, for example.
  • Pieces can "capture" opposing pieces if they move onto a square with an opposing piece; the opposing piece is then taken off the board
  • The goal is to get in a position where you can capture the opposing king - this is called Checkmate and wins you the game
